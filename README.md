# KTP Events

RSVP app for **Kappa Theta Pi, Syracuse University** — brotherhood and social
event tracking with name-attached RSVPs and an archive of what actually pulled
people out.

**Live:** <https://aharve10.github.io/KTP_Events_App/>

Accounts are restricted to `@syr.edu` and `@g.syr.edu` addresses. Officer
actions (creating events, editing thresholds, marking turnout) are gated on a
per-chapter passcode.
